💚 SUPPORT / HEALER
S-Tier

Alok

Drop the Beat

Creates a 5m aura giving +10% move speed and 5 HP/s heal for 5 seconds. Best support in the game.

S-Tier

Dimitri

Healing Heartbeat

Creates healing zone that restores 10 HP/s and allows allies to auto-revive inside it.

A-Tier

K / Captain Rainbow

Psych Professor

EP to HP conversion plus EP regen aura. Has 2 modes you can swap between.

A-Tier

Kapella

Healing Song

Boosts healing items and skills. Slows ally HP loss when downed.

A-Tier

Thiva

Vital Vibes

Revives teammates 70% faster and gives 60 HP on revive. Best for squad ranked.

B-Tier

Olivia

Healing Touch

Revived allies receive extra HP when you revive them.

B-Tier

Notora

Racer's Blessing

Heals the entire squad while riding in vehicles.

B-Tier

Maxim

Gluttony

Uses medkits and mushrooms faster than other characters.

🛡️ DEFENSIVE / TANK
S-Tier

Chrono

Time Turner

Deploys a force field shield that blocks incoming damage and increases movement speed.

A-Tier

Kenta

Swordsman's Wrath

Creates a frontal shield that blocks damage. Shield resets when you attack enemies.

A-Tier

Antonio

Gangster's Spirit

Starts every match with extra HP above the normal maximum.

B-Tier

Andrew

Armor Specialist

Reduces vest durability loss so your armor lasts longer in fights.

B-Tier

Nairi

Ice Iron

Gloo walls become tougher and heal in cold environments.

B-Tier

Ford

Iron Will

Reduces damage taken while outside the safe zone.

⚔️ ATTACKER / RUSHER
S-Tier

Jota

Sustained Raids

Restores 40 HP on SMG or Shotgun kill with 5 second cooldown. Best for aggressive rushers.

A-Tier

Xayne

Xtreme Encounter

Instant shield burst plus kill-based HP recovery. Great for aggressive plays.

A-Tier

Hayato

Bushido

Increases armor penetration as your HP drops. More dangerous when low health.

A-Tier

Jai

Raging Reload

Automatically reloads magazine after knocking down an enemy.

B-Tier

Luqueta

Hat Trick

Maximum HP increases with every kill. Gets stronger throughout the match.

B-Tier

Miguel

Crazy Slayer

Gains EP per kill to sustain longer in fights.

B-Tier

Ryden

Spider Drone

Slows and damages enemies. Reduces enemy movement speed by 80%.

🔍 SCOUT / INTEL
S-Tier

A-Tier

Marks enemies that shoot you, revealing their location briefly.

B-Tier

Shows nearby enemy locations after you get a kill.
