Aggressive

🏃 Rusher

Always push enemies fast. Don't give them time to heal or reload. Use gloo walls to cover yourself while pushing.

Best characters: Chrono, Skyler, Over

Best weapons: M1887, MP40

Passive

🏕️ Camper

Find a safe position and wait for enemies to come to you. Use houses and rocks for cover. Let enemies make mistakes.

Best characters: Maro, Antonio

Best weapons: AWM, SKS

Long Range

🎯 Sniper

Stay far from enemies. Use high ground positions. Aim for headshots. Move after every shot so enemies can't find you.

Best characters: Maro, Rafael

Best weapons: AWM, Kar98

Team Player

🤝 Support

Focus on keeping your squad alive. Always carry medkits. Revive teammates quickly. Use characters with healing abilities.

Best characters: Alok, Dimitri, Thiva

Best weapons: Any assault rifle

Balanced

👑 All Rounder

Can adapt to any situation. Good for ranked mode. Switch between rushing and camping depending on the zone.

Best characters: Alok, Chrono

Best weapons: AK + SKS combo

Clash Squad — short rounds, 4 vs 4, you buy guns, no revive.
Strategy 1

Eco Rush Round 1-2

Save Money First

In round 1 and 2, don't buy expensive guns. Use pistol and grenade and rush together. This lets you buy full guns and armor in round 3. Buying early leaves you poor later.

Strategy 2

Fake One Side, Attack Other Side

Confuse Enemy

Send 3 players to A site. Send 1 player to make noise at B site. The enemy thinks everyone is at B, so your 3 players can attack A fast and break their defense.

Strategy 3

Use Gloo Wall Before Peeking

Stay Safe

Before you go out to shoot, throw a gloo wall first. Then quickly look from the side of the wall and shoot, so enemies can't see and kill you fast.

Strategy 4

Save Your Gun If You Will Lose

Don't Waste Money

Example: it's 2 vs 4 and you have low HP. Don't die and lose your gun — run away and save it. Next round you'll have money to buy a full set again.

Strategy 5

After Planting Bomb, Spread Out

Hold All Angles

After planting the bomb, don't stay together. One player watches the close door, one watches the middle, one watches where the enemy can come from — so a single grenade can't kill you all.

Battle Royale — big map, 50 players, you loot, and you can revive teammates.
Strategy 1

Drop Hot Place or Quiet Place

Loot Fast

Don't drop in middle places. Either drop Clock Tower or Factory to fight early, or drop far houses with no one to loot peacefully. Middle drops usually mean you die with no gun.

Strategy 2

Let Other Teams Fight First

Be The 3rd Team

When you hear 2 squads fighting, don't run in first. Wait until they both have low HP, then push and kill them easily. This is called 3rd partying.

Strategy 3

Take Car Early To Rotate

Don't Die In Zone

In the first 2 minutes, find a car. When the zone is about to close, drive early — don't wait till the last second, or you'll get shot while running in the open.

Strategy 4

Use 3 Gloo Walls In A Fight

3 Wall Rule

Wall 1 to hide from bullets, Wall 2 to climb up high, Wall 3 to run to a new place. Never fight in an open field without a wall.

Strategy 5

Last Zone: Take House On Hill

Play High Ground

When only 3-4 teams are left, find a house on top of a hill. Stay inside, shoot from windows, throw grenades, and let other teams fight each other below you.

• If you like to Rush: use CS Strategy #2 and BR Strategy #2
• If you like to Camp: use CS Strategy #5 and BR Strategy #5
