Aggressive

🏃 Rusher

Always push enemies fast. Don't give them time to heal or reload. Use gloo walls to cover yourself while pushing.

Best characters: Chrono, Skyler, Over

Best weapons: M1887, MP40

Passive

🏕️ Camper

Find a safe position and wait for enemies to come to you. Use houses and rocks for cover. Let enemies make mistakes.

Best characters: Maro, Antonio

Best weapons: AWM, SKS

Long Range

🎯 Sniper

Stay far from enemies. Use high ground positions. Aim for headshots. Move after every shot so enemies can't find you.

Best characters: Maro, Rafael

Best weapons: AWM, Kar98

Team Player

🤝 Support

Focus on keeping your squad alive. Always carry medkits. Revive teammates quickly. Use characters with healing abilities.

Best characters: Alok, Dimitri, Thiva

Best weapons: Any assault rifle

Balanced

👑 All Rounder

Can adapt to any situation. Good for ranked mode. Switch between rushing and camping depending on the zone.

Best characters: Alok, Chrono

Best weapons: AK + SKS combo
