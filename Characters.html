Support

Alok

Creates a healing aura that restores HP and increases movement speed. Best for aggressive squad play.

Rusher

Chrono

Creates a force field that blocks enemy damage. Great for pushing enemies and creating cover.

Support

Dimitri

Creates a healing zone. Players inside can self-revive. Best support character for ranked mode.

Rusher

Skyler

Destroys gloo walls with a sonic wave. Perfect for rushing campers hiding behind walls.

Speed

Over

Increases movement speed after killing an enemy. Perfect for aggressive rushers who want to keep pushing.

Rescue

Thiva

Speeds up rescue time for teammates. Best support character for squad ranked matches.

Damage

Maro

Increases damage with distance. The further the enemy the more damage. Perfect for snipers.

HP Boost

Xayne

Temporarily boosts HP and increases damage to gloo walls. Great for aggressive players.
