Classic Map
🔥 Good For Killing / Rush

Clock Tower

3 houses + roof. Lots of guns. Everyone drops here. Die fast or get 5 kills.

Factory

Big building + lots of loot. 2-3 teams land here always.

Pochinok

Small but 3 teams land. Fast fights.

🏕️ Good For Camping / Safe Loot

Cape Town

5 houses, usually empty. Loot peacefully.

Shipyard

Far edge. 1-2 teams max. Good cars.

Mars Electric

Quiet. 3 houses + zipline to rotate.

Rim Nam Village

Observatory

High ground with decent loot. Quiet start, good for holding angles later.

Sentosa

Volcano Map

Brasilia

8 houses + center. Most teams land here.

The Mill

Quarry

🏕️ Good For Camping / Safe Loot

Command Post

Edge of map. 4 houses. Very quiet.

River Town

Houses by water. Easy to escape with boat.

Marshland

3 houses. Nobody drops here.

Desert Map
🔥 Good For Killing / Rush

Refinery

Center of map. 4 buildings. Best loot = most fights.

Communication Tower

High ground. Snipers love it.

Treasure Spot

2 teams always fight here for the airdrop.

🏕️ Good For Camping / Safe Loot

Dam

Far corner. 2 houses + vehicle.

Radar Station

Ancient Ruin

Quiet. Loot then rotate with zip line.

Sci-fi Map

💡 Pro Tips:
• If you are good and want 10+ kills: Drop hot — Clock Tower, Refinery, Nexus
• If you want to reach Heroic: Drop edge — Cape Town, Dam, Drift Yard. Loot, then 3rd party.
• If you are new: Drop 2nd house from the hot drop. Not center, not edge.
