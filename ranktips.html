Landing

📍 Best Landing Spots

✅ For rank push: Land at edge locations like Rim Nam, Mars Electric, or 200m outside Clock Tower.

✅ For kills: Clock Tower, Peak, Factory. High risk but 4-6 kills early sets you up for top 3.

💡 Rule: If you're still punching at 30s, you landed too greedy!

Fighting

⚔️ When To Attack vs Wait

✅ Push when: You have 3v1 or 4v2, enemy is healing, or you downed one and they're reviving.

✅ Wait when: You hear 2+ teams fighting, low on ammo or gloo walls, or zone is moving away.

💡 Placement beats kills. 1st place with 2 kills beats 5th with 8 kills!

Squad

🤝 Teammate Cooperation

✅ Assign roles: IGL/Caller, Rusher, Support, Sniper.

✅ Good callout: "2 enemies, 60m, northeast rock" not "they're over there".

💡 Never loot a death box while teammate is fighting. Cover first!

Zone

🔵 Zone Tips

✅ Rotate early: Start moving at 1:00 for circles 3-5.

✅ Play the edge of safe zone so only 180° can shoot you.

✅ High ground wins final 3 circles. Use 3-4 gloo walls in zig-zag.

Pro Tips

🔥 Other BR Tips

✅ Meta guns: M1014/UMP close, AN94/SCAR mid, SVD/M82 long.

✅ Best combo: DJ Alok + K + Orion + Wolfrahh.

💡 Warm up 10 min in training. First game aim is always off!

Map Control

📍 Map Control Tips

✅ Factory Bermuda: Control mid with SMG rush. Take mid, cut rotations.

✅ Clock Tower: Hold B-site with 2, flank with 2. Don't all go one site.

💡 First 10s of buy phase decide the round!

Fighting

⚔️ When To Attack vs Wait

✅ Attack when: You win pistol round, 4v3 advantage, or know exact enemy location.

✅ Wait when: You lost pistol round. Force-save, play time.

💡 Don't dry peek. No flash/gloo wall = giving free kills!

Economy

💰 Economy Management

✅ 2 people die round 1? Everyone force-save round 2.

✅ Pistol round = M500 or G18.

✅ Round 2 = UMP if won, save if lost.

✅ Round 3+ = M4A1/AN94 + vest.

Squad

🤝 Teammate Cooperation

✅ Roles: 1 entry SMG, 2 AR mid, 1 support with grenades/gloo walls.

✅ Call HP, location, gun after every fight: "50 HP, M4, A-site".

💡 Lose 2 rounds in a row? Timeout, reset, change strategy!

Pro Tips

🔥 Other CS Tips

✅ CS is 70% crosshair placement, 30% movement.

✅ Use gloo walls to block angles, not to hide behind.

✅ Up 3v2 with 20s left? Play time, let bomb timer run.

🗺️ 1. Battle Royale Modes — Big Map

Classic BR

50 Players
How to play

Drop from plane, loot houses, find guns, stay in safe zone. Last team alive wins. You can revive teammates.

Ranked BR

50 Players
How to play

Same as Classic but you get rank points. Win = more points, lose = lose points. Goal is to reach Heroic.

Lone Wolf BR (Solo)

1 Player
How to play

Only you, no teammates. Drop, loot, fight 49 other solos. Last 1 alive wins.

Duo BR

2 Players
How to play

You + 1 friend vs 24 other teams. Work together — one loots, one watches. Revive each other.

Squad BR

4 Players
How to play

Full team. This is the main mode for rank. Have 1 rusher, 1 support, 1 sniper, 1 IGL to call.

⚡ 2. Clash Squad Modes — Small Map

Classic CS

4 vs 4
How to play

7 rounds. Buy guns each round. First team to win 4 rounds wins. No revive. Plant bomb or kill all.

Ranked CS

4 vs 4
How to play

Same as Classic CS but you get CS rank points. This is the fastest way to Heroic.

Lone Wolf CS (1v1)

2 Players
How to play

Only you vs 1 enemy. First to win 5 rounds wins. No money, same guns. Pure aim.

Lone Wolf Headshot

2 Players
How to play

1 vs 1 but ONLY headshots count. Body shots do 0 damage. Best mode to practice aim.

Big Head CS

4 vs 4
How to play

Same as CS but everyone has a big head. Headshots are easier. Very funny and fast.

Convoy Crunch

4 vs 4
How to play

You are inside cars with guns. Drive and shoot the enemy car. Blow up their car to win the round.

CS Gun King

4 vs 4
How to play

Every round everyone gets the same random gun. No buying. Tests who is best with all guns.

✨ 3. Special Event Modes — Limited Time

Team Deathmatch (TDM)

4 vs 4
How to play

First team to 40 kills wins. Instant respawn. No money. Just keep fighting.

Rush Hour

4 vs 4
How to play

Like CS but super fast. Only pistols and SMG. 3 rounds to win.

Pet Rumble

4 vs 4
How to play

CS but you can use 2 pets. Pets give extra skills in fight.

Zombie Hunt

4 Players vs AI
How to play

You 4 players vs zombies. Survive 10 waves. Collect money to buy guns. Don't die.

Rampage

50 Players
How to play

BR but everyone has special skills and monsters spawn. Kill monsters for loot.
