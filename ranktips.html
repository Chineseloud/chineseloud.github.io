Landing

📍 Best Landing Spots

✅ For rank push: Land at edge locations like Rim Nam, Mars Electric, or 200m outside Clock Tower.

✅ For kills: Clock Tower, Peak, Factory. High risk but 4-6 kills early sets you up for top 3.

💡 Rule: If you're still punching at 30s, you landed too greedy!

Fighting

⚔️ When To Attack vs Wait

✅ Push when: You have 3v1 or 4v2, enemy is healing, or you downed one and they're reviving.

✅ Wait when: You hear 2+ teams fighting, low on ammo or gloo walls, or zone is moving away.

💡 Placement beats kills. 1st place with 2 kills beats 5th with 8 kills!

Squad

🤝 Teammate Cooperation

✅ Assign roles: IGL/Caller, Rusher, Support, Sniper.

✅ Good callout: "2 enemies, 60m, northeast rock" not "they're over there".

💡 Never loot a death box while teammate is fighting. Cover first!

Zone

🔵 Zone Tips

✅ Rotate early: Start moving at 1:00 for circles 3-5.

✅ Play the edge of safe zone so only 180° can shoot you.

✅ High ground wins final 3 circles. Use 3-4 gloo walls in zig-zag.

Pro Tips

🔥 Other BR Tips

✅ Meta guns: M1014/UMP close, AN94/SCAR mid, SVD/M82 long.

✅ Best combo: DJ Alok + K + Orion + Wolfrahh.

💡 Warm up 10 min in training. First game aim is always off!

Map Control

📍 Map Control Tips

✅ Factory Bermuda: Control mid with SMG rush. Take mid, cut rotations.

✅ Clock Tower: Hold B-site with 2, flank with 2. Don't all go one site.

💡 First 10s of buy phase decide the round!

Fighting

⚔️ When To Attack vs Wait

✅ Attack when: You win pistol round, 4v3 advantage, or know exact enemy location.

✅ Wait when: You lost pistol round. Force-save, play time.

💡 Don't dry peek. No flash/gloo wall = giving free kills!

Economy

💰 Economy Management

✅ 2 people die round 1? Everyone force-save round 2.

✅ Pistol round = M500 or G18.

✅ Round 2 = UMP if won, save if lost.

✅ Round 3+ = M4A1/AN94 + vest.

Squad

🤝 Teammate Cooperation

✅ Roles: 1 entry SMG, 2 AR mid, 1 support with grenades/gloo walls.

✅ Call HP, location, gun after every fight: "50 HP, M4, A-site".

💡 Lose 2 rounds in a row? Timeout, reset, change strategy!

Pro Tips

🔥 Other CS Tips

✅ CS is 70% crosshair placement, 30% movement.

✅ Use gloo walls to block angles, not to hide behind.

✅ Up 3v2 with 20s left? Play time, let bomb timer run.
