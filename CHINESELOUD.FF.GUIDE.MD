# Chineseloud.FF.guide Code of Ethics

The Chineseloud.FF.guide community is built on trust, respect, fairness, and cooperation. Everyone who takes part in the community is expected to follow these principles.

## Our Principles

- **Be respectful.** Treat everyone with dignity, even when you disagree.
- **Be honest.** Give accurate information and do not deliberately mislead other members.
- **Be responsible.** Think about how your actions and words may affect the community.
- **Be welcoming.** Help create an environment where new members feel comfortable participating.
- **Be fair.** Do not give unfair advantages or treat people differently without a legitimate reason.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not share another person's private information without their permission.
- **Communicate responsibly.** Avoid unnecessary arguments, harassment, threats, or offensive language.
- **Give credit.** Respect the work of others and acknowledge their contributions when appropriate.
- **Follow the rules.** Follow the community guidelines and the rules of the platforms where Chineseloud.FF.guide operates.
- **Help others.** When possible, support members who need assistance and contribute positively to the community.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
- Threats or encouragement of violence.
- Discrimination or hateful behavior.
- Deliberately spreading false information.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Impersonating another person or organization.
- Deliberately disrupting the community.
- Abusing administrative privileges.
- Repeatedly ignoring community rules.

## Reporting Concerns

If you believe someone has violated this Code of Ethics or the community rules, please report the issue through the community's designated reporting channel.

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith. The Chineseloud Admin Team will review reports and take appropriate action when necessary.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Ethics, please contact the Chineseloud Admin Team.

We expect everyone involved in Chineseloud.FF.guide to help maintain a respectful, safe, and welcoming community.
